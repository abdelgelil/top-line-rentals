## Brand & Style

### Personality & Core Pillars
This design system embodies high-end coastal hospitality and architectural prestige along the Mediterranean shorelines of Alexandria and the North Coast. It positions short- and long-term residences not merely as accommodation, but as curated seaside sanctuaries. The brand personality balances three tenets:
- **Quiet Luxury:** Subdued, confident elegance avoiding cluttered ostentation; spacious proportions, intentional negative space, and immaculate finishing.
- **Architectural Distinction:** Inspired by grand coastal high-rises, waterfront facades, and private keys unlocking premier residences.
- **Bilingual Hospitality:** Effortless fluidity between English and Arabic, maintaining consistent prestige, optical weight, and visual poise across RTL and LTR orientations.

### Visual Style
The design system operates at the intersection of **Contemporary Editorial Minimalism** and **Tactile Luxury**. Surfaces are defined by deep midnight nautical tones contrasted against luminous warm alabaster canvas layers. Gold appears purposefully as structural accents—slender hairline borders, metallic brass badges, and warm luminous highlights—evoking polished brass key cards and illuminated architectural crowns.

### Target Audience & Emotional Impact
Tailored for affluent domestic vacationers, international expatriates, and high-net-worth Gulf travelers seeking uncompromising comfort, sea views, and bespoke concierge hospitality. The visual interface evokes serenity, exclusivity, warmth, and total trustworthiness.

## Colors

The palette directly mirrors the deep nautical twilight of the Mediterranean and polished champagne gold architectural fixtures.

### Primary Palette
- **Champagne Brass (`#C6A052`):** The primary metallic accent. Applied to focal brand elements, active states, key interactive pill buttons, and premier property ratings.
- **Gilded Light (`#DFBE74`):** Secondary highlight hue. Utilized for subtle metallic linear gradients, hover states, and luminous typography glows.
- **Deep Bronze Gold (`#A68032`):** Anchoring border and pressed-state gold, providing optical depth and ground against dark canvas layers.

### Background & Surface Hierarchy
- **Midnight Obsidian (`#080D1A`):** Deepest background tone, establishing an immersive, cinematic backdrop for high-resolution property photography.
- **Nautical Navy (`#0B1325`):** Base container and card elevation surface, providing soft separation from the canvas.
- **Subtle Navy Slate (`#152038`):** Elevated cards, dialog overlays, and secondary interactive components.

### Light & Neutral Accents
- **Warm Pearl / Alabaster (`#F9F8F5`):** Primary display text, hero headlines, and light-mode card surfaces.
- **Muted Sand (`#D4CEBF`):** Secondary typography, captioning, and metadata labels.
- **Hairline Slate (`#1F2E4D`):** Architectural boundary lines, dividing rules, and card borders.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy harmonizes classical Mediterranean resort grandeur with modern digital readability.

### Type Pairings & Script Matching
- **Latin Display:** `Playfair Display` offers statuesque elegance, sharp brackets, and high editorial contrast. Reserved for property names, architectural collections, and hero narratives.
- **Latin Functional:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` delivers crisp, contemporary geometry with open counters, ensuring optimal legibility across booking selectors, dates, technical unit specifications, and pricing metrics.
- **Arabic System Counterpart:** When rendering Arabic locales, substitute `Playfair Display` with high-contrast display Arabic such as `Cairo` (SemiBold / Bold) for titles, and `Tajawal` / `Cairo` (Regular / Medium) for interface body copy to preserve equivalent optical presence and rhythm.

### Typographic Polish
Headlines employ tighter negative letter-spacing to reinforce architectural solidity, while labels, tags, and kicker headers (`label-sm`, `label-md`) are set in uppercase tracking (`+0.04em` to `+0.08em`) to mimic boutique brass room plaques.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Structure
The design system implements a 12-column fluid grid system bounded by a maximum content width of `1440px` for desktop viewports. Spacious section margins prevent visual density, letting property imagery and sea views serve as breathing room.

- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12-column structure with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and `3rem` (48px) margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with `1.25rem` (20px) gutters and `2rem` (32px) margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1.25rem` (20px) outer edge margins.

### Spatial Rhythm
Padding within cards and modules utilizes a deliberate `space-md` (`1.25rem`) and `space-lg` (`2rem`) cadence. High-ticket penthouse listings and suite overviews receive generous `space-xl` (`3.5rem`) vertical section separation, cementing the unhurried "quiet luxury" tempo.

### RTL Grid Symmetries
All spatial vectors use logical CSS properties (`padding-inline`, `margin-inline`, `inset-inline-start`) to maintain identical bilateral equilibrium when switching between English and Arabic layouts.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered deep-water surfaces paired with luminous champagne edge lighting rather than muddy drop shadows.

### Atmospheric Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Foundation):** Midnight Obsidian (`#080D1A`), the endless canvas floor.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Containers):** Nautical Navy (`#0B1325`) with a 1px border stroke (`rgba(198, 160, 82, 0.15)`). Used for listing cards, amenities grids, and booking bars.
- **Tier 2 (Floating Modals & Flyouts):** Navy Slate (`#152038`) with hairline metallic border (`rgba(223, 190, 116, 0.35)`) and an ultra-diffused atmospheric shadow: `0 20px 40px -10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(198, 160, 82, 0.2)`.

### Luminous Edge Highlights
Cards and primary buttons take advantage of inner micro-bevel highlights:
- `box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 rgba(223, 190, 116, 0.25)` gives components an architectural, beveled brass trim.

## Shapes

The interface balances sharp architectural lines with soft, rounded radiuses inspired by the rounded chamfered key emblems and polished archways of luxury coastal compounds.

- **Standard Base Radii (0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to form fields, secondary filters, and calendar cells.
- **Large Container Radii (1rem / 16px):** Property showcase cards, interactive photo galleries, and booking sidebars.
- **Pill Silhouette (`rounded-full`):** Call-to-action buttons, concierge contact ribbons, and luxury status badges (e.g., "Seafront View", "Penthouse"), echoing the key-hole and circular brand badge geometries.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Luxury CTA:** Solid champagne gold background (`#C6A052`) transitioning smoothly on hover to gilded light (`#DFBE74`). Text is rendered in deep midnight navy (`#080D1A`) with bold, medium-tracked Plus Jakarta Sans (`label-lg`). Outer border uses a fine metallic ring with rounded-full pill styling.
- **Secondary Ghost:** Transparent nautical navy surface bordered by a 1px brass stroke (`#C6A052`). Text in warm pearl alabaster (`#F9F8F5`), shifting to gold fill on hover.

### Property Listing Cards
- Deep nautical navy base (`#0B1325`) bordered by a subtle hairline slate-gold edge (`#1F2E4D`).
- Imagery uses a 16:10 aspect ratio with an interior micro-gradient vignette at the bottom edge.
- Price overlays appear in gilded typography (`#DFBE74`) set in `Playfair Display`, paired with muted currency labels.

### Input Fields & Booking Bar
- Low-profile inputs with `#080D1A` fill and 1px `#1F2E4D` border. Focus states activate a crisp `#C6A052` outer glow and gold stroke.
- Datepicker and guest selector dropdowns use dark glassmorphic backdrops (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`) with brass accent borders.

### Status Badges & Amenity Chips
- Pill-shaped tags with semi-translucent midnight fill (`rgba(11, 19, 37, 0.8)`), 1px champagne border (`rgba(198, 160, 82, 0.4)`), and warm alabaster text with micro gold icon prefixes (e.g., Balcony, Sea View, Private Beach).

### Bilingual & RTL Specifics
- Contact bar and phone ribbons mirror seamlessly: Egyptian international phone numbers (`+20 ...`) maintain strict LTR numeral formatting inside bidirectional wrappers.
- Iconography (chevrons, keys, directional arrows) flips dynamically in RTL context while location pins and key ring brand motifs remain upright.
